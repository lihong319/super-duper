How to apply?

Step 1: Navigate to Login Page

Click on "Application" in the navigation menu, which is located within the "CTA" (Call to Action) submenu.

Step 2: Access the Sign-Up Page

On the Login page, provide a "Sign Up" link. When users click on this link, they will be directed to the Sign-Up page.

Step 3: Sign Up for an Account

  • On the Sign-Up page, users should enter their desired username and password.
  • After completing the form, users click the "Submit" button.
  • Upon submission, users will be redirected back to the Login page.

Step 4: Log In with New Credentials

  • Users are required to enter the username and password they just created on the Sign-Up page.
  • Click the "Submit" button to proceed.

Step 5: Complete Registration

  • After successful login, users will access the Registration page.
  • On this page, users should provide their students' details and parent/guardian information. This may include names, contact information, and any necessary academic data.
  • Once all information is entered, users click the "Submit" button to move to the next step.

Step 6: Proceed to Payment

  • On the Payment page, users are presented with options to select the number of subjects they want to enroll in.
  • Users need to make the payment for the selected subjects. This could involve selecting payment methods, entering payment details, and confirming the payment.
  • After a successful payment, users have completed the application process.
