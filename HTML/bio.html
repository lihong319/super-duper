History About Dr. Bi Ou Luo Ji

Dr. Bi Ou Luo Ji holds a Ph.D. in Biology from Harvard University, where he developed a profound understanding of the subject. After graduating, he collaborated with leading researchers Walter Hartwell White at Grey Matter Inc., where their groundbreaking research on crystallization earned them a Nobel Prize. Inspired by one of his colleagues who gravitated towards academia, Dr. Bi Ou accepted a teaching position at the University of Nottingham, specializing in Biology. Today, at the age of 69, Dr. Bi Ou may have aged in body and mind, but his spirit and ambition remain as youthful as ever. Super Duper is honored to have someone as distinguished and accomplished as Dr. Bi Ou on our teaching staff.

About Me

Hello, I'm Dr. Bi Ou Luo Ji, but you can call me Dr. Bi.
