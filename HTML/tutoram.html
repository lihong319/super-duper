About Me

Hello! I'm Mr. Lim, and I love making the world of math accessible and fun for students. I am excited to be your teacher in Addmath and would like to tell you a little about myself.

I have always had a strong interest in mathematics, and this interest led me to pursue a career in teaching. I got an accounting degree from [your university], where I graduated with honors. During my academic journey, I had the opportunity to explore different branches of mathematics and gain a strong foundation in the discipline.

What really sets me apart as a teacher is my commitment to making math meaningful and fun for my students. I understand that many students find math difficult, and I am here to change that perception. My teaching style is based on patience, clarity, and a genuine desire to help my students succeed.

I believe every student has the potential to excel in math, and my goal is to unlock that potential. I tailor my teaching time to each student's individual needs, adapting my teaching style to their learning preferences. Whether you are struggling with algebra, geometry, calculus, or any other math, I am here to offer guidance and support.

Outside of class, I focus on developing student problem-solving skills and developing strong mathematical thinking skills. I encourage my students to ask questions, think critically, and develop a deeper understanding of the subject.

Beside of teaching, I am involved in the world of mathematics by attending workshops, seminars, staying up to date with the latest teaching techniques and products always looking for new and innovative ways to improve mathematics.
