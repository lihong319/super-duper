Our Location

Visit Us

Welcome to our Super Duper Building where all the miracle happens. Nestled on a spacious 2-acre expanse, this futuristic marvel redefines the way education and inspiration converge. Its sleek and avant-garde architectural design stands as a testament to our commitment to fostering a cutting-edge learning environment.

Inside, the Tutor Building unfolds into a realm of possibilities. Vast and well-illuminated corridors guide students through a realm of knowledge and discovery. The heartbeat of practical learning thrives within our state-of-the-art bio labs and other specialized labs, where students engage in hands-on experiments, pushing the boundaries of their understanding.

Located centrally in Petaling Jaya, our Tuition Centre is easily accessible by public transportation and offers a variety of resources to help prospective students make informed decisions for their higher education.
