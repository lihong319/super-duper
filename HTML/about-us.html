From the Interim Provost and CEO

At Super Duper, our journey began with a passion for transforming education and shaping the futures of young minds. Founded in 1998, we embarked on a mission to provide students with a nurturing and innovative learning environment that goes beyond traditional teaching methods.

The idea for Super Duper was born out of a desire to bridge the gap between conventional education and the evolving needs of students. With a deep understanding of diverse learning styles, Willy Won Ka assembled a team of 4 passionate educators who shared the vision of making learning engaging and impactful.

Over the years, we've witnessed countless success stories, students who came to us struggling and left as confident, accomplished, full A's scholars; individuals who once saw challenges as obstacles and now view them as opportunities for growth. These stories fuel our passion and reinforce our dedication to our students' success.

As we look ahead, Super Duper remains steadfast in its commitment to shaping them into leaders, thinkers, and changemakers of tomorrow. We invite you to join us on this journey, where education is not just a means to an end, but a transformative experience that empowers students to reach their fullest potential.

Baron Willy of Won Ka OBE

Meet the visionary behind Super Duper, Willy Won Ka. With a deep passion for education and a wealth of experience in tutoring, Willy Won Ka has dedicated 50 years to shaping young minds and inspiring a love for learning.

Having studied and worked in Harvard, IMU, Oxford and BeijingCorn Inc, Baron Willy honed their skills in teaching, tutoring and writing etc. They have a proven track record of achieving 4.0cgpa in haravrd, being the second youngest person to received the noble Price, which has earned them recognition as a thought leader in the education field.
